# Internship Status Flow

## Overview

Every internship progresses through a series of statuses.

Each status represents the current stage of the internship process and indicates what action, if any, is expected from the student.

Students can monitor these statuses through the Internship Management Portal.

# Status Descriptions

## Draft

### Meaning

The internship registration has been started but not submitted.

### Student Action

Complete the registration form and submit it.

---

## Submitted

### Meaning

The internship application has been submitted successfully.

The application is waiting for Faculty Scrutiny.

### Student Action

Wait for Faculty review.

No further action is required.

---

## Under Scrutiny

### Meaning

Faculty is reviewing the internship application.

The Faculty verifies internship eligibility and submitted information.

### Student Action

Wait until the Faculty approves or rejects the application.

---

## Approved

### Meaning

Faculty has approved the internship registration.

The student can continue with the internship.

### Student Action

Complete the internship successfully.

---

## Rejected

### Meaning

Faculty rejected the internship registration.

A reason for rejection is provided.

### Student Action

Edit the internship details.

Correct the required information.

Resubmit the internship application.

---

## Internship In Progress

### Meaning

The internship has been approved and is currently ongoing.

### Student Action

Continue the internship until completion.

---

## Manager Evaluation Pending

### Meaning

The internship has been completed.

The portal is waiting for the Manager to submit the internship evaluation.

### Student Action

Wait for the Manager to submit the evaluation.

---

## Internal Evaluation Required

### Meaning

The Manager cannot provide an internship evaluation.

Faculty will conduct an Internal Evaluation instead.

### Student Action

Wait for further communication from the Faculty.

Attend the Internal Evaluation if required.

---

## Evaluation Completed

### Meaning

The internship evaluation has been completed.

The internship now proceeds to report submission.

### Student Action

Wait until the Faculty opens the report upload window.

---

## Report Upload Open

### Meaning

Faculty has opened the report upload period.

Students are allowed to upload internship reports.

### Student Action

Upload the internship report before the deadline.

---

## Report Uploaded

### Meaning

The internship report has been successfully uploaded.

### Student Action

No further action is required.

Wait for Faculty review.

---

## Report Under Review

### Meaning

Faculty is reviewing the submitted internship report.

### Student Action

Wait until the review is completed.

---

## Grade Recorded

### Meaning

Faculty has completed the internship evaluation and assigned the internship grade.

The grade is securely stored in the system.

Grades are **not yet visible** to students.

### Student Action

Wait until grades are officially published after the complete internship evaluation process.

---

## Credits Updated

### Meaning

Internship credits have been recorded according to the applicable internship policy.

Credits may be visible in the portal.

### Student Action

Verify the updated internship credits.

No additional action is required.

---

## Grades Published

### Meaning

The internship evaluation process has been completed.

Internship grades are now visible to students.

### Student Action

View the published internship grades.

---

## Internship Completed

### Meaning

The complete internship process has been successfully finished.

### Student Action

No further action is required.

---

# Common Student Questions

## Why is my internship still under scrutiny?

Your internship application is currently being reviewed by the Faculty.

Please wait until the review is completed.

---

## Why is my internship rejected?

Faculty has rejected your application.

Review the rejection reason, edit the application, and resubmit it.

---

## Why can't I upload my report?

The report upload window may not have opened yet or it may have already closed.

Students can upload reports only during the Faculty-configured upload period.

---

## Why are my grades not visible?

Grades are intentionally published only after the complete internship evaluation process is completed after the 8th semester.

---

## Why can I see my credits but not my grades?

Internship credits and internship grades are different.

Credits may be visible according to the internship record.

Grades remain hidden until final publication after the 8th semester.
