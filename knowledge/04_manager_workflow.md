# Manager Workflow

## Overview

The Manager Workflow begins after a student has successfully completed their internship.

Managers are responsible for evaluating the internship performance of students assigned to them.

Managers do not participate in internship registration, approval, grading, or internship credit allocation.

---

# Login

Actor: Manager

Managers log into the Internship Management Portal using their official company email address.

After successful authentication, the Manager is redirected to the Manager Dashboard.

Outcome:

The Manager gains access to the list of assigned students.

---

# Assigned Students

Managers can only view students assigned to them.

Managers cannot:

- View students assigned to other Managers.
- Modify student internship registrations.
- Approve internship applications.

Outcome:

Each Manager only evaluates students under their supervision.

---

# Internship Evaluation

Actor: Manager

After the internship is completed, the Manager reviews the student's internship performance.

The Manager can evaluate aspects such as:

- Technical skills
- Communication
- Problem solving
- Professional behaviour
- Overall internship performance

The Manager submits the evaluation through the Internship Management Portal.

Outcome:

The evaluation is submitted successfully and forwarded for further processing.

---

# Internal Evaluation Request

If the company cannot provide an internship evaluation, the Manager can indicate that Internal Evaluation is required.

In this case:

- The internship is not evaluated by the company.
- The Faculty conducts an Internal Evaluation.
- The Faculty's evaluation is considered for grading.

Outcome:

The internship continues through the Internal Evaluation workflow.

---

# Manager Dashboard

The Manager Dashboard allows Managers to:

- View assigned students.
- View internship details of assigned students.
- Submit internship evaluations.
- Request Internal Evaluation when company evaluation is unavailable.
- Track evaluation submission status.

---

# Manager Permissions

Managers can:

- Log into the portal.
- View assigned students.
- Submit internship evaluations.
- Request Internal Evaluation.

Managers cannot:

- Register internships.
- Approve internship registrations.
- Reject internship registrations.
- Assign academic grades.
- Award internship credits.
- Configure report upload schedules.
- Access Administrator functions.

---

---
# Evaluation Submission Rules

Once a Manager submits an internship evaluation, the submission becomes final.

Managers cannot:

- Edit a submitted evaluation.
- Delete a submitted evaluation.
- Submit another evaluation for the same internship.

If an incorrect evaluation is submitted, any further action must be handled by the Faculty or the Department according to departmental procedures.
---
# Notifications

Managers may receive notifications regarding:

- Pending internship evaluations.
- Evaluation submission reminders.
- Successful evaluation submission.
