# Internship Report Upload

## Overview

After the internship evaluation process is completed, students are required to upload their Internship Report through the Internship Management Portal.

The report submission process is controlled entirely by the Faculty.

Students can upload reports only during the report upload period configured by the Faculty.

---

# Purpose

The Internship Report serves as an official record of the student's internship work.

Faculty reviews the report along with the internship evaluation before assigning the final internship grade.

---

# Report Upload Workflow

Student completes internship

↓

Manager Evaluation

OR

Internal Evaluation

↓

Faculty opens Report Upload window

↓

Student uploads Internship Report

↓

Faculty reviews the report

↓

Grade Assignment

---

# Report Upload Schedule

Faculty configures:

- Report Upload Start Date
- Report Upload End Date

Students can upload reports only within this period.

Once the upload window closes, report submission is disabled.

---

# Student Responsibilities

Students are responsible for:

- Uploading the internship report before the deadline.
- Ensuring the report is complete.
- Uploading the correct report.

Students should verify that the upload is successful before the submission deadline.

---

# Faculty Responsibilities

Faculty members are responsible for:

- Opening the report upload window.
- Setting the upload deadline.
- Reviewing uploaded reports.
- Using the report during internship evaluation.

---

# Upload Rules

Students can upload reports only when:

- Internship evaluation has been completed.
- The report upload window is open.

Students cannot upload reports:

- Before the upload window opens.
- After the upload deadline.
- If report submission has already been closed.

---

# Report Status

Possible report statuses include:

## Report Upload Pending

The report upload window has not opened yet.

Student Action:

Wait until Faculty opens the upload window.

---

## Report Upload Open

The report upload window is currently open.

Student Action:

Upload the internship report before the deadline.

---

## Report Uploaded

The internship report has been uploaded successfully.

Student Action:

No further action is required.

Wait for Faculty review.

---

## Report Under Review

Faculty is reviewing the uploaded report.

Student Action:

Wait until the review is completed.

---

## Report Submission Closed

The report upload deadline has passed.

Student Action:

Contact the Faculty if assistance is required.

---

# Frequently Asked Questions

## When can I upload my internship report?

Only after the Faculty opens the report upload window.

---

## Who decides the report upload dates?

The Faculty configures the report upload schedule.

---

## Can I upload my report after the deadline?

No.

Reports cannot be uploaded after the report upload window has closed.

---

## What happens after I upload my report?

Faculty reviews the report as part of the internship evaluation process before assigning the internship grade.

---

## Can I upload my report before completing my internship?

No.

Report upload becomes available only after the internship evaluation stage is completed and the upload window is opened.
