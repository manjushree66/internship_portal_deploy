# Internship Management Portal

## Overview

The Internship Management Portal is a web-based application developed for the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at PES University.

The portal provides a centralized platform to manage the complete internship lifecycle, including internship registration, faculty scrutiny, approval, manager evaluation, internal evaluation, report submission, grading, and internship credit management.

The portal is designed to simplify the internship process for Students, Faculty, Managers, and Administrators while ensuring compliance with the official internship guidelines issued by the department.

---

# Purpose

The Internship Management Portal aims to:

- Digitize the internship management process.
- Replace manual internship registration with a secure online portal.
- Enable students to register and track internships.
- Allow Faculty to perform scrutiny and approve internship registrations.
- Allow Managers to evaluate student internships.
- Support Internal Evaluation when company evaluation is unavailable.
- Allow students to upload internship reports.
- Enable Faculty to review reports and assign grades.
- Maintain internship records and monitor student progress.
- Award internship credits after successful completion of the evaluation process.

---

# Users

The portal supports four different user roles.

## Student

Students can register internships, monitor approval status, upload internship reports, view notifications, and track the progress of their internship.

## Faculty (Scrutiny)

Faculty members perform internship scrutiny, approve or reject internship registrations, configure report upload schedules, conduct Internal Evaluation when required, review internship reports, assign grades, and award internship credits after the 8th semester evaluation.

## Manager

Managers evaluate assigned students after internship completion. If company evaluation cannot be provided, the Manager can request Internal Evaluation.

## Administrator

Administrators manage the portal, maintain Faculty accounts, and publish portal announcements.

# Official Guidelines

The portal follows the official Internship Guidelines issued by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering.

The Internship Guidelines define academic rules such as internship eligibility, internship credits, evaluation policies, internship duration, and NOC procedures.

The portal implements these guidelines through an online workflow.
