# User Roles

## Overview

The Internship Management Portal supports four different user roles.

Each role has different responsibilities, permissions, and workflows within the system.

The four user roles are:

- Student
- Faculty (Scrutiny)
- Manager
- Administrator

Only authorized users can access their respective dashboards after logging into the portal.

---

# Student

## Purpose

The Student is the primary user of the Internship Management Portal.

Students use the portal to register internships, monitor internship progress, upload reports, and track internship completion.

## Responsibilities

A Student can:

- Log into the portal using SRN and password.
- Register internship details.
- View internship approval status.
- Track internship progress.
- Upload internship reports during the report upload period.
- View internship grades.
- View internship credits after the final evaluation process.
- Receive notifications regarding internship activities.

## Student Cannot

A Student cannot:

- Approve internships.
- Modify internship evaluations.
- Assign grades.
- Change report upload schedules.
- Award internship credits.

---

# Faculty (Scrutiny)

## Purpose

Faculty members act as the Scrutiny Authority of the Internship Management Portal.

They verify internship registrations, supervise the internship process, conduct Internal Evaluation when necessary, review reports, assign grades, and award internship credits.

## Responsibilities

Faculty can:

- Review internship registrations.
- Approve internship registrations.
- Reject internship registrations.
- View student internship details.
- Configure report upload schedules.
- View uploaded internship reports.
- Conduct Internal Evaluation.
- Assign internship grades.
- Award internship credits after the 8th semester evaluation.
- Monitor internship progress.

## Faculty Cannot

Faculty cannot:

- Submit Manager evaluations on behalf of a company.
- Modify student login credentials.
- Access Administrator functions.

---

# Manager

## Purpose

Managers evaluate the internship performance of students working under their supervision.

## Responsibilities

Managers can:

- Log into the portal using their official company email.
- View only the students assigned to them.
- Submit internship evaluations.
- Indicate that Internal Evaluation is required if company evaluation cannot be provided.

## Manager Cannot

Managers cannot:

- Approve internships.
- Assign academic grades.
- Award internship credits.
- View students assigned to other Managers.

---

# Administrator

## Purpose

Administrators maintain the Internship Management Portal and manage overall portal operations.

## Responsibilities

Administrators can:

- Manage Faculty accounts.
- Publish announcements.
- Maintain portal information.
- Manage portal access.

## Administrator Cannot

Administrators cannot:

- Perform Faculty scrutiny.
- Submit Manager evaluations.
- Conduct Internal Evaluation.
- Assign internship grades.

---

# Role Summary

| Role | Primary Responsibility |
|------|-------------------------|
| Student | Register internships and track progress |
| Faculty | Scrutiny, approval, evaluation, grading and credits |
| Manager | Internship evaluation |
