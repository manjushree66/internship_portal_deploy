# Internship Credit System

## Overview

The Internship Management Portal follows the official internship credit policy issued by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering.

Every student must successfully complete a total of **8 internship credits** before graduation.

Credits may be earned through different internship combinations depending on the student's internship plan.

---

# Total Internship Credits

Every student must complete:

**Total Internship Credits = 8**

Students can earn these credits using different valid combinations supported by the Internship Management Portal.

# Important Rules

Every student must complete a total of **8 internship credits**.

Students are free to choose any valid internship combination supported by the department.

If internships are completed in both the 4th and 6th semester, only the better summer internship is considered for academic credits.

Students cannot receive more than **8 internship credits**.

---

# Credits vs Grades

Internship Credits and Internship Grades are different.

Credits indicate the academic credit earned for internship completion.

Grades represent the student's performance in the internship evaluation.

Credits may become visible in the portal according to the applicable internship record.

However, internship grades are **not displayed immediately**.

Grades become visible only after the complete internship evaluation process is finished after the 8th semester.

# Frequently Asked Questions

### How many internship credits are required?

Every student must complete **8 internship credits**.

---

### Can I complete only an 8th semester internship?

Yes.

A student may complete all **8 internship credits** during the 8th semester.

---

### Can I complete internships in both the 4th and 6th semester?

Yes.

However, only the better summer internship is considered for academic credits.

---

### Will I receive 4 credits if I complete both the 4th and 6th semester internships?

No.

Only one summer internship is considered.

The better internship is selected.

---

### Why are my grades not visible?

Grades are intentionally published only after the complete internship evaluation process has finished after the 8th semester.

---

### Why can I see credits but not grades?

Credits and grades are different.

Credits may be visible according to the internship record, while grades remain hidden until final publication after the 8th semester.
