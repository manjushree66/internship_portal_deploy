# Faculty Workflow

## Overview

Faculty members act as the Scrutiny Authority of the Internship Management Portal.

They supervise the complete internship lifecycle from internship registration until final internship completion.

Faculty members ensure that internship registrations comply with the official internship guidelines and departmental policies.

Faculty members also conduct Internal Evaluation whenever company evaluation is unavailable.

---

# Faculty Responsibilities

Faculty members are responsible for:

- Reviewing internship registrations.
- Approving or rejecting internship applications.
- Monitoring internship progress.
- Configuring internship report upload schedules.
- Conducting Internal Evaluation.
- Reviewing internship reports.
- Assigning internship grades.
- Awarding internship credits according to the official internship policy.
- Monitoring the complete internship lifecycle.

---

# Step 1 - Faculty Login

Actor: Faculty

Faculty members log into the Internship Management Portal using their Faculty credentials.

After successful authentication, they are redirected to the Faculty Dashboard.

Outcome:

Faculty gains access to all internship management features.

---

# Step 2 - Internship Scrutiny

Actor: Faculty

Faculty reviews every internship registration submitted by students.

The Faculty verifies:

- Company information
- Internship duration
- Internship type
- Student details
- Manager information
- Eligibility according to internship guidelines

Outcome:

The internship application proceeds for approval or rejection.

---

# Step 3 - Internship Decision

Faculty has two options.

## Option 1 - Approve

The internship registration is approved.

The internship status changes to:

Approved

The student receives an approval notification.

The student can proceed with the internship.

---

## Option 2 - Reject

The internship registration is rejected.

Faculty provides the rejection reason.

The student edits the application.

The student resubmits the internship.

The application returns to Faculty Scrutiny.

---

# Step 4 - Internship Monitoring

Faculty can monitor internship progress throughout the internship duration.

Faculty can view:

- Internship details
- Student information
- Internship status
- Manager details
- Evaluation status

---

# Step 5 - Report Upload Schedule

Faculty configures the internship report upload window.

The Faculty specifies:

- Report upload start date
- Report upload end date

Students can upload reports only during this period.

Uploads outside the configured window are not accepted.

Outcome:

The report submission process becomes available to eligible students.

---

# Step 6 - Manager Evaluation Review

After internship completion, Faculty waits for the Manager's decision.

Two possibilities exist.

## Company Evaluation Submitted

The Manager submits the internship evaluation.

Faculty reviews the submitted evaluation.

The internship proceeds for report review and grading.

---

## Internal Evaluation Required

If the Manager cannot provide an evaluation, the Manager selects Internal Evaluation Required.

Faculty schedules and conducts an Internal Evaluation.

The Faculty evaluates the student's internship performance.

The Faculty's evaluation replaces the company evaluation.

Outcome:

The internship evaluation process is completed.

---

# Step 7 - Internship Report Review

Faculty reviews the internship report uploaded by the student.

Faculty verifies:

- Internship work
- Report completeness
- Report quality
- Internship outcomes

The report review contributes to the overall internship assessment.

---

# Step 8 - Grade Assignment

Faculty evaluates all available information, including:

- Manager Evaluation

OR

- Internal Evaluation

and

- Internship Report

Faculty assigns the internship grade.

The assigned grade is stored in the system.

Grades are **not displayed immediately** to students.

Grades become visible only after the complete internship evaluation process concludes after the 8th semester.

Outcome:

Internship grading is completed.

---

# Step 9 - Internship Credits

Faculty awards internship credits according to the official internship policy.

Credits are recorded based on:

- Internship duration
- Internship eligibility
- Internship completion
- Official internship credit rules

Credits may be visible for completed internships according to the applicable internship record.

Internship grades remain hidden until final publication after the 8th semester.

---

Faculty Dashboard provides access to:

- Internship registrations
- Student details
- Internship status
- Pending approvals
- Approved internships
- Rejected internships
- Pending evaluations
- Internal Evaluations
- Report upload schedule
- Uploaded reports
- Grade assignment
- Internship credits

---

# Faculty Permissions

Faculty can:

- Approve internships
- Reject internships
- Configure report upload schedule
- Conduct Internal Evaluation
- Review internship reports
- Assign grades
- Award internship credits
- View internship details
- Monitor internship progress

Faculty cannot:

- Modify Manager evaluations after submission.
- Modify Administrator settings.
- View Administrator credentials.

- Pending approvals
- Pending evaluations
- Internal Evaluation requests
- New report uploads
- Pending grading tasks
