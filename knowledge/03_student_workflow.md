# Student Workflow

## Overview

The Student Workflow describes the complete internship journey from registration to the final award of internship credits.

Students can monitor every stage of their internship through the Internship Management Portal.

---

# Step 1 - Login

Actor: Student

The student logs into the Internship Management Portal using their SRN and password.

After successful authentication, the student is redirected to the Student Dashboard.

Outcome:
The student gains access to internship-related features.

---

# Step 2 - Internship Registration

Actor: Student

The student registers an internship by filling in the Internship Registration Form.

The student provides details such as:

- Company Name
- Internship Role
- Internship Type
- Internship Duration
- Semester
- Manager Name
- Manager Official Email
- Additional internship details required by the portal

After verifying the information, the student submits the registration.

Outcome:
The internship application status becomes **Submitted**.

---

# Step 3 - Faculty Scrutiny

Actor: Faculty

The Faculty reviews the submitted internship application.

The Faculty verifies:

- Internship eligibility
- Company details
- Internship duration
- Required information

The Faculty can either:

- Approve the internship
- Reject the internship

---

# If Approved

The internship status changes to:

Approved

The student receives a notification.

The student can now continue with the internship.

---

# If Rejected

The internship status changes to:

Rejected

The student can view the reason for rejection.

The student edits the internship details and resubmits the application.

The application is sent back for Faculty Scrutiny.

---

# Step 4 - Internship Completion

After completing the internship, the student waits for the Manager Evaluation process.

The student does not perform any action during this stage.

---

# Step 5 - Manager Evaluation

The Manager evaluates the internship.

Two possible outcomes exist.

## Option 1

The Manager submits the internship evaluation.

The internship proceeds to the Report Upload stage.

## Option 2

The Manager is unable to evaluate the internship.

The Manager selects **Internal Evaluation Required**.

The Faculty then conducts an Internal Evaluation.

After Internal Evaluation is completed, the workflow continues.

---

# Step 6 - Internship Report Upload

The Faculty opens the Report Upload window.

The student uploads the Internship Report during the allowed upload period.

Report uploads outside the allowed period are not accepted.

After successful upload, the internship status becomes:

Report Uploaded

---

# Step 7 - Report Review

Faculty reviews the uploaded internship report.

The report is evaluated together with the Manager Evaluation or Internal Evaluation.

---

# Step 8 - Grade Assignment

Faculty evaluates the internship and assigns the internship grade.

The assigned grades are stored by the system.

Internship grades are not displayed to students immediately.

Grades become visible only after the complete internship evaluation process is finished during the 8th semester.

Outcome:

The internship evaluation is completed and the grade is recorded.

---

# Step 9 - Internship Credits

Internship credits are recorded based on the completed internship and the applicable credit policy.

Credits earned from internships may be visible for internships completed during the 4th, 6th, or 8th semester.

However, internship grades are published only after the final internship evaluation process is completed during the 8th semester.

The final internship result follows the official internship credit policy.

# Student Dashboard

The Student Dashboard allows students to:

- Register internships
- View internship details
- Track internship status
- Upload internship reports
- View grades
- View internship credits
- Receive notifications
